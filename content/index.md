Welcome to the world of **Antiquary**. Here you will find (*almost*) everything that you need to know about the game

## What is Antiquary?

**Antiquary** is a rogue-like deck builder, similar to [Slay the Spire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/646570/Slay_the_Spire/) and it's sequel. Assemble a deck of cards in order to take down your enemies and gather the required relics to perform the **Sacrifice of the Self** and reach apotheosis
