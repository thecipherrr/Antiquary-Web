## Definition

- "Phantom" cards generated by other cards
- These cards do not count toward the maximum hand size
- These cards cannot be moved to other piles except to be played
- After played, the cards are [Destroyed](Destroy.md)

## Rules Clarification

- Transient cards function as "extra cards" and as such, they do not count towards the maximum hand size
- They are kept in a separate place from the players' hand
- Cannot be moved to other piles include:
	- Discarded
	- Exhausted
	- 
## Used By

- [[Sanguine Spikes]]

## System Role

- Allow for card generation
- No movement to other piles stops game breaking combos and bugs
